Architecture for Stock Price Forecasting," AAAI 2024, pp. 8389-8397.

Released source: https://github.com/SJTU-DMTai/StockMixer

original authors. Project additions include component-removal and recurrent
variants, exact experiment records, checkpoint/resume support, and execution
optimizations. Historical execution hashes are retained in run metadata. Publication comments,
docstrings, and diagnostic messages have been standardized without changing the
computation. `PUBLICATION_MANIFEST.json` maps original and publication hashes.
